## Brand & Style

The design system is crafted for high-velocity, empathetic food rescue logistics. It blends the wholesome craft of local neighborhood food culture (bakeries, grocers, community markets) with the urgent, transparent efficiency of emergency relief dispatch.

### Core Character
- **Vital & Wholesome:** Communicates peak freshness, nutritional dignity, and respect for food donors and recipients alike.
- **Urgent without Panic:** Balances ambient calm (organic forest tones) with high-visibility kinetic indicators for perishable rescue windows.
- **Tactile & Welcoming:** Avoids sterile logistics tropes through generous radii, friendly typography, and warm culinary accents.

### Design Movement
**Tactile Organic Modernism:** Combines crisp whitespace and clean geometric typography with pill-shaped micro-elements, soft atmospheric shadows tinted with warm amber and moss, and pronounced surface layer contrast that makes key actions feel tangible and rewarding on touch screens.

## Colors

The color palette centers on lush, vitality-rich greens anchored by warm bakery ambers and high-visibility urgency reds.

### Palette Architecture
- **Primary (`#15803D` - Harvest Green):** The core brand tone symbolizing renewal and safety. Deeper tones (`#14532D`) anchor solid interaction states, while tint tones (`#86EFAC`, `#DCFCE7`) provide reassuring backgrounds for matched claims and completed rescues.
- **Secondary (`#D97706` - Artisan Ochre):** Evokes fresh crusts, warm kitchens, and logistical transit. Complemented by `#F59E0B` for active listings and `#FFFBEB` for warm surface containers.
- **Tertiary (`#DC2626` - Critical Window Red):** Reserved exclusively for time-decay triggers (e.g., pickups closing in under 30 minutes, temperature-sensitive cold chain notices).
- **Neutrals (`#0F172A` Slate Base):** Charcoal text hierarchy against bleached porcelain (`#FFFFFF`) and chilled pantry off-whites (`#F8FAFC`, `#F1F5F9`). Avoid pure black to maintain an organic, approachable feel.

## Typography

The type system relies on **Plus Jakarta Sans** across all levels. Its balanced geometric construction and slightly rounded terminal curves yield high legibility at glance speed while projecting warmth and neighborly community spirit.

### Typographic Hierarchy Rules
- **Display & Large Headlines:** Set with tight tracking (`-0.02em` to `-0.03em`) and heavy weights (`700` or `800`) to create authoritative visual anchors on donation dashboards.
- **Numbers and Quantities:** Always paired with `label-lg` or `headline-md` tabular figures to ensure crate counts, poundage, and countdown clocks do not visually wobble during real-time updates.
- **Labels & Microcopy:** `label-sm` utilizes uppercase formatting with loosened letter spacing (`0.04em`) when applied to cold-chain verification and perishable status flags.

## Layout & Spacing

A mobile-first, 4-column layout model with dynamic viewport scaling dictates the application layout.

### Grid & Layout Rules
- **Mobile Handheld (360px - 480px):** 4-column fluid layout with `16px` outer margins and `12px` gutters. Floating action zones maintain safe bottom padding of `84px` to accommodate the physical home indicator and pinned navigation bar.
- **Tablet & Split Terminal (768px - 1024px):** 8-column layout with `24px` margins and `16px` gutters, allowing side-by-side rescue logs and donor verification maps.
- **Vertical Rhythm:** Rooted in an 8pt base grid. Component paddings consistently utilize `space-sm` (`8px`) for compact operational chips and `space-md` (`16px`) for cards and input containers.

## Elevation & Depth

Visual hierarchy uses **tonal depth and organic ambient shadows** rather than hard structural lines.

### Elevation Levels
- **Level 0 (Floor Canvas):** Flat `#F8FAFC` background. Completely shadow-free.
- **Level 1 (Listing Cards, Input Shells):** Elevated surface `#FFFFFF` layered on `#F8FAFC`. Shadow: `0 2px 8px -2px rgba(15, 23, 42, 0.04), 0 1px 3px -1px rgba(21, 128, 61, 0.03)`. Subtle green ambient tint reinforces organic tone.
- **Level 2 (Hover/Grabbed Cards, Action Sheets):** Shadow: `0 10px 24px -4px rgba(15, 23, 42, 0.08), 0 4px 8px -2px rgba(217, 119, 6, 0.05)`. Amber-tinted penumbra signals tactile readiness for claim actions.
- **Level 3 (Modal Alerts & Bottom Drawers):** Shadow: `0 20px 32px -8px rgba(15, 23, 42, 0.16)`. Accompanied by a 24% dark slate scrim with `backdrop-blur(4px)` for environmental context.
- **Countdown Micro-Depth:** Urgency elements and closing countdown indicators receive a soft glowing halo (`0 0 12px rgba(220, 38, 38, 0.25)`) when time falls under 30 minutes.

## Shapes

The design system employs **Rounded (Level 2)** geometry with selective pill contours for fast thumb-reach actions.

### Radius Distribution
- **Cards & Surface Modules:** Bound with `rounded-2xl` (`16px`) and `rounded-3xl` (`24px`), softening content tiles and reducing visual harshness.
- **Buttons, Badges & Chips:** Completely pill-shaped (`rounded-full` / `9999px`) to create high-affordance, punchy interactive items that stand distinct from square packaging images.
- **Inputs & Dropdowns:** `rounded-xl` (`12px`) for a comfortable, balanced touch target.

## Components

### Buttons
- **Primary Rescue Action:** Solid Harvest Green (`#15803D`) with bold white text. Pill-shaped (`rounded-full`), min-height `52px`, `px-6`. Active state scales down gently (`scale(0.98)`) and shifts to Deep Moss (`#14532D`).
- **Urgent Claim Action:** Amber Ochre (`#D97706`) background with white text, or pure Sunset Crimson (`#DC2626`) for immediate dispatch pickups.
- **Secondary / Ghost:** `#FFFFFF` background, `1.5px` border in `#E2E8F0`, dark slate label. Press state triggers `#F1F5F9`.

### Chips & Status Badges
- **Perishable Tags (Bakery, Produce, Prepared Meals):** Pill-shaped, small uppercase label. Tinted organic backgrounds (e.g., `#FEF3C7` for baked goods, `#DCFCE7` for farm produce) with matched dark label ink.
- **Urgency Countdown Meter:** Dual-part component featuring a clock icon and live ticking countdown. 
  - Standard window: Ochre tint (`#FFFBEB` background, `#B45309` text).
  - Critical window (<30 mins): Pulsing crimson pill (`#FEE2E2` background, `#DC2626` text, animated red dot indicator).

### Cards (Donation Listing)
- Pure white container (`rounded-2xl`), nested in 8pt internal padding, bordered by a soft `1px` translucent edge (`rgba(15, 23, 42, 0.05)`).
- Contains hero thumbnail with rounded corners (`rounded-xl`), donor bakery/market title, distance badge, and a high-contrast weight/item count indicator.

### Input Fields
- Generous `48px` minimum height, `rounded-xl`, `#FFFFFF` surface with `#CBD5E1` border.
- Active focus state transitions to `#15803D` border with a subtle 3px ring (`rgba(21, 128, 61, 0.15)`). Placeholder copy is warm slate (`#94A3B8`).

### Checkboxes & Segmented Controls
- **Checkboxes:** `20px` squares with `rounded-md` (`6px`). Checked state uses `#15803D` with a clean white stroke checkmark.
- **Logistical Category Tabs:** Pill-shaped sliding container with `#F1F5F9` background track and an elevated `#FFFFFF` white thumb indicator showing active selection.

### Custom Component: Rescue Impact Meter
- Visual progress tracker showing pounds of rescued food, metric tons of CO2 averted, and families supported. Features high-saturation emerald fills over sage track lines (`#86EFAC` on `#DCFCE7`), paired with milestone badges.
